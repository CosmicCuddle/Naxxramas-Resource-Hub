How this calculator works

Talent points: one point per level beginning at 10. Vanilla allows 51 at level 60; TBC 61 at level 70; Wrath 71 at level 80.

Row access: The original custom 3.3.5 talent trees are unchanged. Vanilla displays rows 1–6 in full and only each tree's main capstone in row 7. TBC displays rows 1–8 in full and only the main capstone in row 9. Wrath shows all 11 rows and every talent. These are position limits only—not historical expansion release-date restrictions. A higher row still requires five points per previous row.

Talent requirements: arrows, prerequisite ranks and the 3.3.5 spell/talent IDs are taken from your supplied DBC files. Missing references are flagged rather than guessed.

Sharing: builds save their talent IDs and ranks in the link, so recipients can open them without creating an account. Local saved builds stay in this browser.

About the data: Talent icons are mapped from your server's SpellIcon.dbc, with specialization artwork behind each talent tree. Most tooltip effect values are resolved from your current Spell.dbc. Durations and some special formula values still require additional client data, so these are labelled instead of guessed.
