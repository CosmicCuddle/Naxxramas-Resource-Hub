# Naxxramas application review — legitimate-interests assessment (release working record)

**Date:** 4 November 2026  
**Controller:** CosmicCuddle (owner/operator of the private Naxxramas WoW server)  
**Contact:** naxxapplicationteam@gmail.com  
**Status:** Working assessment based on owner-approved application rules. **The owner approved the legitimate-interests approach in principle**; this record is not professional legal advice or an independent compliance certification. Reassess if the scope, audience, providers or data practices change.

## 1. Purpose test: what interest is being pursued?

Naxxramas is a small, privately operated World of Warcraft community with manual joining decisions. The owner needs to review prospective members' intentions and gaming interests, communicate a decision, and arrange the next steps for people who are accepted. Manual screening is intended to support community cohesion and help prevent abusive or unsuitable joining requests, rather than to profile people for advertising.

**Provisional finding:** This is a concrete, non-commercial legitimate interest connected to running a private gaming community.

## 2. Necessity test: why use this information?

The approved **three-page Word form** requests a preferred future account username, a reply email, an optional Discord username, and responses to 17 numbered joining/gameplay questions. A username helps arrange an account after acceptance; an email address is needed for a reply; experience and interests help make the owner-selected community decision. Discord details are optional. A manual decision cannot be made in the owner's intended way without some answers; the owner-approved questionnaire is used instead of a separate duplicate online form.

**Less intrusive alternatives considered:** Open, immediate sign-up would collect less application information but would not deliver the owner's selected close-knit/manual-admission process. A second online questionnaire is unnecessary and was explicitly rejected. Applicants should be told they do **not** have to give a real name, password, payment information or unnecessary sensitive details. Questions not needed for admission should remain optional; do not ask for unrelated personal information.

**Provisional finding:** The requested ordinary gaming and reply details are proportionate to a manual application flow, provided the form is operated as described and non-essential information is not required.

## 3. Balancing test: applicant interests, reasonable expectations and safeguards

- **Expectations:** Applicants intentionally download and submit a form to join a private server. The website gives a clear privacy notice **before** the Tally upload. No automated admission decision is promised.
- **Privacy impact:** The application may reveal contact details, preferred name/username and game interests. Accidental disclosure, unauthorised file sharing and excessive retention are the main foreseeable risks. Do not invite or use sensitive-category information.
- **Access & handling:** The owner privately reviews responses using an owner-controlled Tally account; completed applications and applicant answers are not posted on GitHub Pages, in public GitHub Issues, or in public community channels. No marketing use is authorised. Do not share tokenised file-upload links publicly.
- **Retention:** An undecided application is decided or closed/deleted within **90 days of submission**. Following an acceptance or decline, delete the Tally submission and owner-held copies within **30 days of that decision**, including emptying Tally Trash. Provider backups may have separate deletion timeframes. An independently minimal onboarding-status record, if needed, is not permission to keep the completed application.
- **Control:** Applicants may withdraw or object to this processing, or request access, correction, restriction or deletion where applicable. The sole published privacy/support route is **naxxapplicationteam@gmail.com**. Replies are manual.
- **Children / vulnerable applicants:** The service's public eligibility/age arrangements are not established in these records. If applications are expected from children, apply extra scrutiny and revisit the balancing test before collecting their data. This assessment does **not** establish an age-verification system.

**Provisional finding:** For voluntary applications containing only ordinary gaming/contact information and governed by the safeguards above, the owner's interest appears capable of outweighing the relatively limited privacy intrusion. This depends on the stated controls actually being followed. Review any unexpected sensitive information separately and minimise it.

## 4. Decision and remaining operational actions

The owner accepted the **legitimate-interests privacy approach** in the chat on 4 November 2026, but such acceptance does **not** independently verify that every legal or technical requirement is met.

**Before accepting real applications, ensure:** (a) the public Apply privacy notice is reachable; (b) the **actual Tally form** includes the approved email-only privacy/contact instructions and a link to the full public notice; (c) the owner-controlled Tally form is intentionally opened; (d) the approved deletion/Trash process is followed in operation. The owner has closed further *functional testing* of Tally; updating its published wording is a content/release action, not a repeat test.

**Review trigger:** Change of admission purpose, form questions/required fields, processor, retention period, applicant audience (especially children), or any indication of privacy harm.
