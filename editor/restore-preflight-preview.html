How a future restore should behave

Matching draft: continue to a separate review and full source check, not an automatic save.

Older or conflicting draft: keep the draft intact, reopen the latest website version, review the differences, and only then make a fresh draft.

Invalid draft: do not import it; keep the current work untouched.
