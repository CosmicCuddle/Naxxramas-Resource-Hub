Naxxramas is built around fair play, respect and enjoying the Warcraft journey together. Read every rule before joining. All twelve rules below are fully visible, and the server owner makes final decisions about moderation.

Rule 1 — Respect Other Players

Treat others with respect in public and private chat, guilds, dungeon groups, raids and battlegrounds. Harassment, bullying, threats, targeted insults and discrimination are forbidden, including racism, sexism, homophobia, etc.

Friendly differences about strategy, loot or performance are part of group play. Disagreement becomes unacceptable when a player is repeatedly targeted, intimidated, humiliated or threatened. Do not organise others to harass someone or retaliate with further abuse.

Repeated insults, threats and targeted harassment are not acceptable. If discussion breaks down, stop and use a support ticket.

Rule 2 — Do Not Exploit Bugs or Unintended Mechanics

Do not deliberately use bugs, loopholes or broken mechanics to obtain an unfair advantage. This includes item/currency duplication, repeated unintended quest rewards, vendor-pricing errors, abnormal instance resets, enemy-pathing defects, broken progression gates or inaccessible areas.

The client allowing an action does not prove that the action is permitted. A one-off accidental discovery is different from deliberate exploitation. Once you recognise a defect, stop benefiting from it, record enough information to describe it and privately alert the owner.

Example: Repeatedly using a quest turn-in that grants unintended extra rewards is prohibited. Discovering the problem once and reporting it without further farming is the correct response.

Rule 3 — Use Playerbots for Legitimate Gameplay

Playerbots are supported Naxxramas companions. They may fill genuine group roles as tanks, healers or damage dealers for normal questing, dungeons, raids, battlegrounds and supported activities when humans are unavailable.

Playerbots are not an unattended farming workforce. Do not exploit their AI, command systems or scaling to repeat trivial-content reward farms, avoid travel, skip content gates or manufacture unfair loot, gold or Honour. The rule is about abusive purpose, not merely having bots in a party.

Example: Bringing supported Playerbots to an appropriate dungeon so you can complete it is permitted. Repeatedly using an overlevelled bot group to farm an easy dungeon for effortless gold is not.

Rule 4 — Dungeon Farming and Group Fairness

Groups made entirely of human players may farm lower-level dungeons, even if their characters substantially outlevel them. Legitimate repeated runs are not prohibited simply because the content is easy. Actual bugs, duplication and required-encounter bypasses are still forbidden.

A party containing Playerbots, even one bot alongside human players, must not repeatedly farm trivial or outlevelled content for easy gold, items or other rewards. Playerbots should help characters play content in its intended spirit, not remove meaningful challenge so rewards can be extracted repeatedly.

Example: Five real level-45 characters farming Scarlet Monastery Graveyard for gold is allowed if they use legitimate mechanics. Replacing group members with level-45 Playerbots to repeat that trivial reward farm is not. A mixed group tackling suitable content normally remains allowed.

Rule 5 — Report Bugs Instead of Exploiting Them

If you find a bug, suspicious reward, unexpected Playerbot behaviour, quest error or possible loophole, stop exploiting it and report it to the owner through an in-game Game Master support ticket.

Describe the zone or dungeon, relevant character, approximate time and observed quest, item or behaviour. Do not repeatedly recreate an exploit for rewards, encourage others to use it or publish detailed abuse instructions. Never include account passwords in a ticket.

Example: If a boss repeatedly gives incorrect loot, report the behaviour rather than farming it again for gain. A good-faith private report is welcome.

Rule 6 — Play PvP and Battlegrounds Fairly

Earn Honour, objectives and battleground rewards through ordinary, genuine play. Win trading, arranged outcomes, manipulating the opposing faction, intentional inactivity solely to collect rewards or abusing a battleground bug are prohibited.

Supported Playerbots may participate in their intended battleground role, but they must not be used to fix results, farm rewards while players are absent or exploit AI behaviour. Normal defeat, a weak team or a strategy disagreement is not evidence of cheating.

Example: Trying to win a fair battleground is permitted. Agreeing with the other team to trade wins, or remaining inactive to farm Honour, is not.

Rule 7 — Protect the Server Economy

You may buy and sell at the Auction House and trade with other players. Prices may rise or fall according to supply and demand; ordinary price fluctuations, profits and legitimate farming are permitted.

Do not create unfair gold or items through duplication, broken vendor values, auction loopholes or unintended Auction House Bot interactions. Do not deliberately deceive other players in trades. Vanilla bot listing filters are complete; bot-generated TBC and WotLK listings remain under testing, without automatically restricting player-created auctions.

Example: Listing a scarce material at a higher market price is legitimate. Repeatedly exploiting a broken vendor price or duplication bug to generate gold is not.

Rule 8 — Do Not Grief or Abuse Loot Systems

Do not deliberately sabotage parties, disrupt others for spite, manipulate loot settings or deny other players or Playerbots a fair chance at eligible rewards. Ordinary dungeon groups should preserve the standard Need, Greed, Disenchant where available, and Pass choices.

Free for All loot is prohibited. Do not switch to Free for All to take every drop, race others to loot or bypass fair rolls. Master Looter is permitted only in genuine organised raids using the owner's separate approved raid-loot procedure. It is never permitted in normal dungeons, including ten-player dungeons or a dungeon group marked as a raid. The precise authorised raid procedure is not provided here and should be confirmed with the raid organiser.

Example: Turning on Free for All so a leader can collect everything is prohibited. So is using Master Looter in a five-person or ten-person dungeon. A genuine raid may use its separately approved procedure; this is not permission for unfair distribution.

Rule 9 — No Unauthorised Gameplay Automation

External programs and scripts that play unattended, automatically farm, gather, fight, move or acquire rewards without legitimate participation are not permitted. Automated manipulation of other players or systems is likewise prohibited.

Supported Playerbots, approved addons and ordinary in-game macros are not automatically banned. Their use must remain within permitted gameplay. An authorised tool is not a defence for using it to bypass progression, farm unattended or exploit another weakness.

Example: An approved informational addon is fine. An external script that repeatedly plays and farms your character while you are away is not.

Rule 10 — Do Not Bypass Required Dungeon Mechanics

Do not exploit terrain, enemy pathing, Stealth, summons, evasion or unintended routes to bypass required dungeon encounters or intended progression restrictions. Deliberately combining class abilities to transport a party past fights it must complete is prohibited.

Ordinary class Stealth, legitimate Warlock summons and sensible avoidance of optional enemies are allowed when used as intended. The rule does not mean every optional monster must be killed; it concerns bypassing required encounters or mechanics.

Example: A Rogue stealthing past required fights and summoning the rest of a dungeon group beyond those encounters is not allowed when done to avoid required mechanics. Avoiding optional trash along a legitimate route is not automatically a violation.

Rule 11 — Alt Bots and Normal Travel

You may group and level your own alternate characters through the supported Playerbot/altbot system. Altbot levelling is an intended feature, not a violation by itself.

Do not use bot-specific teleportation, instant repositioning or movement shortcuts to bring an alt across the world faster, skip intended travel, evade dungeon paths or bypass expansion or progression requirements. Ordinary legitimate travel and class abilities are allowed. Ask the owner about exceptional stuck-bot recovery rather than assuming a shortcut is acceptable.

Example: Taking a flight path or travelling normally with an altbot is allowed. Using a bot teleport command just to save travel time is not.

Rule 12 — Need, Greed, Disenchant and Pass

Choose Need for a genuine equipment upgrade or another real use by the receiving character, including an appropriate profession ingredient you actually need or a recipe you can learn and use. Choose Greed when the main purpose is resale, profit or lower-priority collection. Choose Disenchant when the option exists and enchanting materials are preferred, or Pass if you do not want the item.

Do not click Need on everything merely to take loot before another player or Playerbot. Simply having a profession does not entitle you to Need on every related material. Discuss contested Bind-on-Equip items, off-spec equipment, scarce recipes and profession materials before rolling. No Free for All and no dungeon Master Looter, including ten-player dungeons. Raids follow their separate approved loot setup.

Example: A healer Needing a genuine healing upgrade is appropriate. Needing unrelated gear just to vendor it is not. If two players genuinely need the same profession material, discuss it and allow a fair roll.

Vanilla Group Etiquette — A Quick Guide

Before the first pull: Say hello, discuss who will tank and heal, agree on the run's route and state any contested item your character genuinely needs.

During a dungeon: Let the tank organise pulls, give healers time to recover, stay with the group and do not rush others into unwanted fights. Keep normal Need/Greed/Disenchant/Pass rolls available. Free for All and Master Looter are not appropriate dungeon loot modes.

For disputed loot: Ask before Needing off-spec gear, valuable Bind-on-Equip items or profession materials someone needs for a specific craft. If a disagreement occurs, talk calmly rather than manipulating settings or sabotaging the run. Organised raids use their own approved loot arrangements.

How to Submit an In-Game Support Ticket

In the original WoW 3.3.5a interface, use the red question-mark (?) Help button near the right side of the bottom menu. Find the available option to contact a Game Master or open a ticket. Describe what happened, where it occurred and relevant characters, items or quests, then submit the ticket.

Never include account passwords or unrelated private details. Menu wording and availability can depend on the server's installed configuration, so report a missing ticket option rather than assuming a command.

Rule Enforcement and Bans

Rule enforcement is considered case by case by the server owner. The type of incident, severity, whether it happened previously and other relevant circumstances matter. Temporary bans can begin at 30 minutes and extend to hours, days or weeks. No automatic penalty ladder is promised.

The existing Temporary Banned section remains part of the Resource Hub. Any future public ban display should show only appropriately approved character names, a reason, status and remaining duration, without exposing login credentials or private account identifiers. The planned ban-data integration is not represented here as already working.

Client Setup & Joining

How do I set up the game client?

Install World of Warcraft 3.3.5a (build 12340) using the Resource Hub's Game Clients information. With the game closed, put the required Naxxramas patch-V.mpq and patch-Z.mpq files into your client's Data folder. Check the New Player Guide and current patch releases for the remaining steps.

Where can I find the WoW client?

Start with the Resource Hub's Game Clients page and choose a compatible WoW 3.3.5a client. The same build 12340 client is used throughout Vanilla, TBC and Wrath progression; a modern retail client will not work.

Where should I install the patches?

Close WoW, then put patch-V.mpq and patch-Z.mpq into the game's Data folder without renaming them. Follow the current release notes if they call for clearing the client cache or other steps.

Are the optional patches required?

No. Optional visual and interface modifications are separate from the documented required server patches. Read each optional patch's description before installing it; do not mix files from another server's client.

Where do I get the server connection details?

If your application is accepted, the owner sends the genuine Naxxramas connection file and instructions privately with your acceptance email. The server address and prepared connection file are not published on this website.

Can I join without applying?

Naxxramas is a private community with a planned application and manual acceptance process. Applying does not automatically create an account or grant access. Follow Apply to Join when applications open; submissions are currently disabled.

What if the client will not connect?

Check the 3.3.5a build, required MPQ files and private connection instructions sent to accepted players. If it still fails, contact the owner with the error text and what you tried. Do not share your account password.

Individual Progression & Tiers

What does Individual Progression (IP) do?

IP tracks progression separately for each character. As you complete milestones, more quests, dungeons, raids and expansion content become available. Most fresh characters begin at Vanilla Tier 0; Blood Elves, Draenei and Death Knights have different starting progression rules. The journey continues through The Burning Crusade and Wrath of the Lich King, with no permanent server-wide level-60 cap.

Is a tier the same as my character level?

No. A tier is a content milestone; a character level measures experience gained. Reaching a level does not automatically complete a progression requirement or unlock the next raid.

What does Tier 0 mean?

Tier 0 is the normal starting Individual Progression state for most fresh characters, before any major Vanilla milestone is completed. It does not mean character level zero. Blood Elves, Draenei and Death Knights are exceptions with separate starting rules; their exact starting stage depends on the character type and server configuration.

What do the Vanilla tiers mean?

A tier is a content milestone, not a character level. For most newly created characters, the journey starts at Tier 0, followed by Vanilla milestones for Molten Core (1), Onyxia (2), Blackwing Lair (3), Ahn'Qiraj preparation and war events (4–5), Ahn'Qiraj (6), and original Naxxramas (7). These numbers describe progression milestones completed; they do not mean every raid unlocks immediately or that a tier number equals a character's level.

What do Vanilla Tiers 1–3 represent?

The module associates Tier 1 with Molten Core, Tier 2 with Onyxia and Tier 3 with Blackwing Lair milestones. The tier number describes progression; other access conditions can still apply.

What do Vanilla Tiers 4–7 represent?

Tiers 4–5 cover Ahn'Qiraj preparation and the war effort, Tier 6 the Ahn'Qiraj milestone, and Tier 7 the original Naxxramas milestone. Specific encounters and prerequisites still matter.

How do I know which tier my character is in?

Your tier is determined by that character's completed Individual Progression milestones, not simply your level. A normal-player tier display or command has not yet been confirmed for this server. If you are unsure, use an in-game Game Master support ticket to ask staff to check your progression. We will add exact player-facing steps once verified.

How do I reach the next tier or expansion?

Complete the required progression activities for your character's current stage. Reaching a particular level alone does not necessarily unlock the next raid or expansion. See the New Player Guide and existing dungeon and raid handbooks; if you believe a requirement has not registered, submit a support ticket instead of bypassing it.

Why is a quest, dungeon or raid unavailable?

Your character may not have the appropriate tier or another required prerequisite. Check the Dungeon Handbooks and your past milestones first. If access seems wrong, send a ticket instead of bypassing the restriction.

Does the server stay at Vanilla and level 60 forever?

No. Characters can progress from Vanilla to The Burning Crusade and then Wrath of the Lich King as they meet the requirements. The level range changes by stage; level 60 is not a permanent server-wide cap.

Will a new alt share my main character's tier?

No. Individual Progression is tracked separately for each character. Your main and a newly created alt can therefore be in different stages; check each character's content access when grouping.

Playerbots & Altbots

What are Playerbots, and how do they work?

Playerbots are computer-controlled characters that can help fill tank, healer or damage roles in group activities. Some bots roam the world; you may also group with supported bots or your own alternate characters as altbots. You do not have to use bots, and normal progression, travel and loot rules still apply.

Are Playerbots compulsory?

No. You can group with other people or play alone where the game allows. Bots are an optional way to fill missing roles.

What is an altbot compared with a roaming Playerbot?

An altbot is one of your own alternate characters taking part through the supported bot system. Other Playerbots may roam the world independently. Their exact commands and quest behaviour need not be identical.

How do I invite or control a bot?

Use the supported Playerbot features available in-game. The exact player controls, commands and limits on this installation have not yet been verified, so this FAQ does not invent a command. If you need help, contact staff.

Do Playerbots accept and finish their own quests?

The underlying Playerbots system supports independently questing roaming bots, but actual behaviour depends on the server's configuration. Bots accompanying a player may help with quest objectives, while quest acceptance, syncing and turn-ins can behave differently. The precise Naxxramas setup is being verified, so do not assume every bot automatically completes your quests for you.

Will Playerbots finish my quests for me?

Bots may help with combat and some objectives, but that does not guarantee that every quest is shared, credited or turned in for your own character. Check your quest log and complete the hand-ins you still need.

Can I level with my own altbots?

Yes. You may bring your alternate characters into a group through the supported bot system and level together. Travel normally: do not use bot-only teleport or instant-reposition shortcuts to skip travel or progression requirements. The Alt Bots and Normal Travel rule explains the restriction.

Can Playerbots help in dungeons, raids and battlegrounds?

Supported bots can fill useful roles in these activities, subject to server settings and progression requirements. Fair-loot rules and any separate genuine-raid loot arrangements still apply.

May I teleport my altbot to save time?

No. Bot-only teleports and instant movement shortcuts cannot be used to avoid normal travel or progression. Use legitimate routes and class abilities, and ask staff if a bot genuinely becomes stuck.

Can I repeatedly farm easy dungeons with Playerbots?

No. A group containing Playerbots must not repeatedly farm trivial or outlevelled dungeons for effortless rewards. Human-only groups may legitimately farm easier content without exploits. See Rule 4.

Loot, Trading & Everyday Play

When should I choose Need, Greed, Disenchant or Pass?

Choose Need for a genuine upgrade or relevant profession use, Greed for resale or lower priority, Disenchant for materials if available, or Pass if you do not want the item. Discuss contested off-spec items, recipes and materials before rolling.

Can my group use Free for All or Master Looter?

Free for All is prohibited. Master Looter is not allowed in normal dungeons, even ten-player dungeons. Genuine organised raids may use only the owner's separately approved raid-loot procedure.

What does the Auction House Bot do?

It helps stock the Auction House; it is a market system, not a companion bot. Players can still trade normally, and prices respond to supply and demand. Vanilla bot-generated listing filters are complete; later-expansion bot listings remain under testing.

Can an all-human group farm an easy dungeon?

Yes. Human-only groups can repeat easier dungeons using legitimate mechanics. The restriction concerns bot-assisted trivial reward farming and actual exploits, not ordinary farming with friends.
