1. Individual Progression — your character's journey

Naxxramas uses Individual Progression. Most fresh characters begin at Vanilla Tier 0, then progress through Vanilla tiers 1–7, The Burning Crusade and Wrath of the Lich King. Blood Elves, Draenei and Death Knights have special starting progression rules instead of the usual Tier 0 start. A tier is a content milestone, not a character level.

Progression requirements determine when particular quests, dungeons, raids and expansion content become available. There is no permanent server-wide level-60 cap: the level range depends on your character's current era and progress. Later expansion stages are unlocked through the relevant individual requirements.

If content is unavailable, check your progression information before assuming something is broken. Exact future-stage access rules and specialist services are still being verified; do not use shortcuts to bypass the requirements.

2. Game client and required patches

Use World of Warcraft 3.3.5a, build 12340. An ordinary 3.3.5a installation alone does not include the server's custom content.

  1. Obtain a compatible 3.3.5a client using the Resource Hub's Game Clients information.
  2. Close the game before moving or replacing patch files.
  3. Download patch-V.mpq from main patch v1.0.6.8.4 and patch-Z.mpq from supporting patch v1.0.6.7.
  4. Place both MPQ files in the WoW client's Data folder, keeping the exact filenames. Check the latest release notes before replacing an older patch.
  5. Clear the client's Cache directory when release instructions require it. Do not delete your Interface or WTF folders; those contain addons and settings.

Visual-only optional patches are not substitutes for the required custom patches. Check Patch Notes and Change Notes for later changes.

3. Connecting after acceptance

The private realm address is not published on this site. If accepted, you receive the approved realm connection configuration file as an attachment to your acceptance email, together with your next steps. Do not use guessed realm addresses or unofficial public connection files.

For the original client, locale-specific connection settings belong within the appropriate client directory, for example WoW/Data/enGB/ or WoW/Data/enUS/. Back up the existing connection configuration first. You can then install the privately attached file or follow the private instructions to edit your existing one. Preserve the correct file extension; do not accidentally add .txt.

Account setup or permission instructions are supplied privately after review. Never send your password through an application, support ticket, Discord message or email.

4. Playerbots, altbots and group play

Supported Playerbots can help fill tank, healer or damage roles so you can enjoy quests, dungeons, raids and other supported activities when human players are unavailable. You can also level your own alternate characters together using supported altbot features. Bots are optional.

They are not a substitute for normal travel or progression. Do not use bot-only teleport/reposition abilities to move alt characters faster or bypass travel routes, dungeon mechanics or eligibility checks. Repeated trivial-content gold or item farming with bot-assisted groups is not permitted.

Ordinary all-human groups may farm easier, lower-level dungeons using legitimate mechanics. A mixed group containing Playerbots must still respect the bot-assisted farming restriction. See the Server Rules for examples.

5. Auction House, trade and loot

Buying and selling

Players can both buy and sell through the Auction House. Bot-generated auctions supplement the market. Prices rise and fall with supply and demand—a rare item may cost more, while common items can be cheaper. Do not exploit auction, vendor or duplication bugs.

Vanilla Auction House Bot listing filters are complete. Bot-generated listings from TBC and WotLK remain under testing; this statement does not restrict ordinary player-created auctions.

Fair loot

Need only for an actual character upgrade or relevant profession recipe/material you genuinely intend to use; Greed for resale or lower-priority wants; Disenchant where available for materials; Pass otherwise. Discuss contested Bind-on-Equip, off-spec or profession items before rolling. Never Need on everything just to prevent bots or other players winning items.

Free for All loot is prohibited. Master Looter is only for genuine organised raids following the owner's separate approved raid-loot setup. It is not allowed in normal dungeons, even a ten-player dungeon.

6. Guides, addons and in-game help

Visit Class Handbooks, Dungeon Handbooks, Module Information and the Talent Calculator for guidance. The N Addon Collection provides server addons; addons are separate from required client patches.

To report a bug or ask about a rule, use the original WoW 3.3.5a interface: click the red question-mark (?) Help button on the bottom menu, find the Game Master ticket option and describe the issue, location and character involved. Never include login credentials.

Getting Started & Client Setup

What kind of server is Naxxramas?

Naxxramas is a private WoW server using AzerothCore, custom systems and Individual Progression. Rather than everyone moving expansions together, your character follows its own Vanilla, The Burning Crusade and Wrath of the Lich King journey. You use the WoW 3.3.5a client throughout.

Which game client and patches do I need?

Use World of Warcraft 3.3.5a, build 12340, with the required patch-V.mpq and patch-Z.mpq. Close WoW before installing both MPQ files in its Data folder, keeping their filenames. Find download links and release guidance in the Game client and required patches section.

Are the optional patches necessary?

No. Optional appearance or interface modifications are separate from the required Naxxramas custom patches. Install one only if you want its stated effect and it is compatible with the client. Check the Patch Notes and Change Notes before replacing files.

How do I receive the private server connection file?

After the owner accepts your application, the approved connection file and setup instructions arrive as a private email attachment. No realm connection address is published on the website. Follow those instructions for your client's language folder and never share your account password.

What should I do if my client will not connect?

Check the 3.3.5a build, both required patches and the private connection instructions you received after acceptance. Take note of the exact error rather than randomly changing files. If you still cannot connect, contact the owner with the error text, but never disclose your password.

Understanding Individual Progression

What does Individual Progression (IP) actually do?

IP tracks each character's completed content milestones and uses them to determine parts of the world, quests, instances and expansion access. It also restores or adjusts some older-era content. Your journey depends on what your own character has earned, not the highest stage another player has reached.

Is a progression tier the same as a character level?

No. A tier is a content milestone, while a character level measures experience. You can reach a suitable level for an instance without meeting its progression prerequisites. Level 60 is not a permanent server-wide cap; later eras have their own ranges and requirements.

How do I check my character's current tier?

Progress is recorded from completed milestones, not only your level or the last boss you fought. A normal-player tier display or command has not yet been verified on this server. If you are unsure, send an in-game Game Master ticket and ask staff to check your earned stage and eligible content.

Does my new alt inherit my main character's progress?

No. IP is tracked per character, so your main and a new alt can be at very different stages. You may play supported altbots together, but that does not automatically equalise their progression. Blood Elves, Draenei and Death Knights also have special starting rules.

Why is a zone, quest, dungeon or raid unavailable?

You may need an earlier milestone, attunement, quest or expansion permission. Check the Dungeon Handbooks and your character's progress before assuming a bug. If access seems wrong, submit a support ticket rather than teleporting or exploiting your way past a restriction.

Vanilla — Tier 0 to Tier 7

Where does a fresh Vanilla character begin?

Most newly created characters start at Tier 0, before a major Vanilla milestone has been completed. Tier 0 does not mean character level zero. Blood Elves, Draenei and Death Knights are exceptions with separate starting progression rules; their exact starting stages depend on the server's effective configuration.

What do Vanilla Tiers 1, 2 and 3 represent?

In the module's milestone records, 1 relates to Molten Core, 2 to Onyxia and 3 to Blackwing Lair. These labels describe progression milestones rather than character levels. Separate raid entrances, quests or other eligibility checks may still apply.

What do Vanilla Tiers 4, 5, 6 and 7 represent?

Tiers 4–5 relate to Ahn'Qiraj preparation and the war effort. Tier 6 is associated with Ahn'Qiraj progression and Tier 7 with the original level-60 Naxxramas milestone. A tier number does not guarantee that every related encounter is immediately accessible.

Can I experience the original Vanilla raids on a 3.3.5a client?

Yes. Individual Progression is designed to restore or adjust older content, including the original level-60 Naxxramas, separately from the later Wrath version. You still use the 3.3.5a client throughout. Check the Resource Hub's guides for the appropriate encounters and prerequisites at your current stage.

What happens after I complete Vanilla progression?

The IP fork recognises the original 40-player Kel'Thuzad milestone as Vanilla's final stage. The owner has approved a planned system in which completing it makes you eligible to purchase TBC passage from Chronomancer Vezrath. That combined milestone-and-payment gate is still being developed, so don't assume it is already enabled in-game.

The Burning Crusade — Outland & Tiers

What changes when I reach The Burning Crusade?

TBC is the next era of your Individual Progression journey, bringing Outland quests, dungeons and raids with their own access requirements. You keep the same 3.3.5a client. Being a suitable character level alone does not necessarily mean that every TBC zone or instance is unlocked.

What progression stages cover the main TBC journey?

The fork uses stages 8–12 as its main TBC progression band. Each number represents a content milestone, not a character level. The boundary after TBC has special historical behaviour, so a stage number alone is not reliable proof of paid access to Wrath.

What do early TBC stages 8 and 9 represent?

Stage 8 is associated with early raid content such as Karazhan, Gruul's Lair and Magtheridon's Lair. Stage 9 relates to Serpentshrine Cavern and Tempest Keep. Other quests or attunements can still apply before you enter an individual raid.

What do later TBC stages 10, 11 and 12 represent?

Stage 10 is associated with Hyjal Summit and Black Temple, and stage 12 with Sunwell Plateau. Stage 11 is not an ordinary active milestone in the fork's progression enum; Zul'Aman has separate eligibility handling. Do not assume every consecutive number opens another raid.

Are TBC heroic dungeons and raids gated?

Some TBC attunement and access content is restored by IP. That means the right level is not always enough; an encounter may also require quest or other progression conditions. Check the relevant Dungeon Handbook and in-game requirements. Exact rules can vary with the server's installed settings.

How does my character advance from TBC to Wrath?

The fork records Kil'jaeden as its final TBC milestone. Under the owner's planned system, you would then need separate character-specific passage from Chronomancer Vezrath; the kill alone would establish eligibility, not paid access. That gate is still under development and testing, so ask staff about the current live process.

Wrath of the Lich King — Northrend & Tiers

Can I enter Northrend automatically at level 70?

Not necessarily. Wrath is a later progression era and entry can depend on completing the preceding expansion's requirements. The owner's planned paid-passage safeguard is not yet verified as live. Do not assume reaching level 70 or finding an accessible portal is permission to bypass progression.

Which raids begin the Wrath progression journey?

The fork associates the early Wrath stage with Wrath Naxxramas, the Eye of Eternity and the Obsidian Sanctum. Wrath Naxxramas is different from the original Vanilla level-60 raid. Your character must still satisfy the appropriate stage and any encounter-specific requirements.

What do Wrath stages 14, 15 and 16 represent?

The module's reference sequence associates 14 with Ulduar, 15 with Trial of the Crusader and 16 with Icecrown Citadel. These are progression reference values; they do not mean those raids automatically open at the instant you receive the number. Check the current raid requirements.

What do Wrath stages 17 and 18 represent?

Stage 17 is associated with the Ruby Sanctum, while 18 is the final recorded Wrath progression milestone in this fork. Content eligibility may still depend on separate conditions. The number describes recorded progress, not an independent character-level bracket.

Why is the TBC-to-Wrath boundary sometimes called stage 13?

The original IP fork uses stage 13 for a late TBC milestone and also historically checks it for early Wrath content. The owner is developing a separate paid expansion-entitlement system to avoid treating those as the same thing. Until verified live, stage 13 alone must not be treated as proof of Wrath access.

Do Death Knights, heroics and emblems work differently in Wrath?

Death Knights have special starting progression rules, so their starting stage must not be guessed from ordinary Tier 0 characters. IP also includes changes to Wrath emblem progression; heroic dungeons and later raids may have additional restrictions. Confirm the current requirements through the handbooks or staff rather than assuming everything opens immediately.

Playerbots & Your Other Characters

Do I have to use Playerbots?

No. They are optional computer-controlled characters that can fill tank, healer and damage roles in supported activities when humans are unavailable. They do not override content access or fair-play requirements, and you can still play with other people instead.

Do roaming Playerbots accept and complete their own quests?

The underlying Playerbots code can perform real quest accept, completion and reward actions. However, exact autonomous questing on Naxxramas has not yet been verified. A bot is not guaranteed to find, accept or turn in every quest; its AI, the quest and the installed configuration all matter.

Do bots automatically share quest credit and turn-ins with me?

Some quests can be shared or coordinated with bots, but the exact results depend on quest behaviour and server settings. Being grouped with a bot does not guarantee that it completes your objectives or receives the same rewards. Always check your own quest log and progress.

Can I level several of my own characters as altbots?

Yes. The supported altbot system allows you to group and level your own alternate characters together. Each one still has its own IP progression record, and group eligibility may depend on expansion requirements. Use normal travel; bot-only teleports are not a permitted shortcut.

Can Playerbots join dungeons, raids and battlegrounds?

Supported Playerbots can help fill roles in these activities, subject to the installed system and content requirements. They may not behave exactly like human players. Dungeon loot rules still apply even if your group is marked as a raid, while genuine raids follow their approved arrangements.

Can I use Playerbots for easy dungeon farming or faster travel?

No. Repeated trivial-content reward farming with a group containing Playerbots is prohibited, as are bot-only teleports used to skip normal travel. Entirely human groups may legitimately repeat easier dungeons using normal mechanics. The important distinction is fair play rather than simply having a bot in a party.

Loot, Trading & Getting Help

How does the Auction House Bot work across expansions?

Players can buy and sell normally, while the Auction House Bot helps provide listings. Prices rise and fall with supply and demand. Vanilla bot-generated listing filters are complete; TBC and Wrath bot-generated filters remain under testing. This does not prevent legitimate player-created auctions.

When should I select Need, Greed, Disenchant or Pass?

Choose Need for a real equipment upgrade or relevant profession use, Greed for resale or lower-priority items, Disenchant where available and preferred, or Pass if you do not want the item. Discuss contested recipes, Bind-on-Equip items and off-spec equipment before rolling.

Are Free for All and Master Looter allowed?

Free for All is prohibited. Master Looter is also forbidden in normal dungeons, including ten-player dungeons. Genuine organised raids may follow the owner's separately approved raid-loot process. Do not change group settings to bypass fair rolling.

What should I do if a quest or progression milestone seems broken?

Stop benefiting from any suspected exploit and report the issue privately. A useful support ticket includes the character, zone, quest or encounter, what happened and approximately when. Staff can check whether you missed a requirement or encountered a real defect.

Where can I find guides or send a Game Master ticket?

Use the Resource Hub's Class Handbooks and Dungeon Handbooks for details. In the original 3.3.5a interface, look for the red question-mark Help button and its Game Master ticket option. Never put login credentials in a ticket.
