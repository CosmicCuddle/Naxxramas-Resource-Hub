What Happens Next?

The server owner will personally review applications and decide whether Naxxramas is suitable for the applicant. Submitting a form is not automatic acceptance and does not create a game account.

When the review is complete, the owner will reply by email, and will also reply through Discord when the applicant provided an optional Discord username. No fixed review turnaround is promised.

If accepted, the player receives the next steps privately, including the approved connection configuration as an attachment to the acceptance email. Passwords are never requested in the application.

Apply to Join — Frequently Asked Questions

These are anticipated applicant questions. Applications are not yet open.

Why do I need to apply?

Our hope is for Naxxramas to become a close-knit, welcoming community where players get to know one another, enjoy their time together and treat each other with respect. An application helps us learn what you enjoy about World of Warcraft and find people who are a good fit for both the server and the community we want to build. It is not a test of how experienced you are.

Do I need years of WoW experience?

No experience test is part of the planned application. We mainly want to understand your interests and what you enjoy about World of Warcraft.

Do I have to provide a real name or password?

No. The planned form asks for a community nickname and an email address. It does not request your real name, game password or account login details.

Is Discord required?

No. Email is required as the main reply channel. Providing a Discord username is optional and allows an additional Discord reply.

Which application file do I upload?

Use the officially approved blank form offered here when applications open, then save and upload your completed document. PDF and editable Word documents are draft options pending the owner's final choice.

When and how will I receive a reply?

The owner reviews each application manually and replies by email. If you provided Discord details, the owner also contacts you through Discord. There is no guaranteed response time.

How is the private connection file delivered?

If accepted, you receive the genuine approved connection file as an attachment to your private acceptance email. It will not be offered as a public download.

Will my application appear publicly?

No public application listing is planned. Before submissions open, the owner must verify private reviewer-only access, a privacy notice, and a manual retention/deletion procedure.

Before you apply
