Application Privacy and Your Information

Naxxramas is a private World of Warcraft community operated by CosmicCuddle. The server owner is responsible for how application information is used. You can contact the owner at naxxapplicationteam@gmail.com with any application, support or privacy question.

What we collect and why

The official Word application asks for your preferred account username, email address, an optional Discord username and answers about your World of Warcraft experience and interests. We use this information to review your request to join, contact you with a decision and help arrange onboarding if accepted. The email address is needed to respond; Discord is optional. Do not include your real name, passwords, payment details or unnecessary sensitive information.

How decisions are made

Applications are reviewed by the server owner. Acceptance is a manual decision, not an automatic result of uploading a file. We rely on the owner's legitimate interests in reviewing voluntary requests to join and maintaining a welcoming private gaming community. This means reviewing only the application information reasonably needed to make a manual decision, rather than using it for advertising or publishing it. You can object to this use of your information by contacting us.

Who handles your application

Your completed application is processed and stored by a third-party form service on behalf of the server owner, who may download a copy for private review. The upload service and relevant email providers process submission and notification information. We do not publish completed applications or answers on the website or in public community spaces. Read the form provider's privacy and data-processing information for details about storage and transfers.

How long we keep it

The owner normally aims to respond within one or two days, but this is not guaranteed. An undecided application will be decided or closed within 90 days of submission; an application closed without a decision will be deleted by that deadline. After an acceptance or decline decision, the owner will delete the submitted application and any copies they hold within 30 days of the decision, including permanently clearing it from the form provider's deleted-submissions area. The provider may temporarily retain backups under its deletion procedures, so full removal from backups may not be immediate. A minimal separate record of acceptance and joining status may be needed for onboarding, but it does not require keeping the original Word application.

Your choices and contact

You may withdraw an application or ask to access, correct or delete information you provided. You can also raise an objection or request that processing be restricted, where applicable. For privacy requests, please email naxxapplicationteam@gmail.com. This is the published contact method for application privacy, withdrawal and data requests. You may also raise a data-protection concern with the UK Information Commissioner's Office where applicable. Do not email completed application documents or passwords; use the private upload area for finished applications once it opens.

Before submitting: Please read this notice. Downloading the blank form does not submit an application. An account is not created automatically, even after submitting the completed form.

What Happens Next?

The server owner will personally review applications and decide whether Naxxramas is suitable for the applicant. Submitting a form is not automatic acceptance and does not create a game account. Accounts are arranged manually after acceptance.

When the review is complete, the owner will reply by email, and will also reply through Discord when the applicant provided an optional Discord username. No fixed review turnaround is promised.

If accepted, the player receives the next steps privately, including the approved connection configuration as an attachment to the acceptance email. Passwords are never requested in the application.

After acceptance: You have one calendar month from your acceptance notification to show you still intend to join. You can confirm in whichever way suits you: reply to your acceptance email, send the owner a Discord message, join the Naxxramas Discord server, or log in to the WoW server. Any one of these is enough when the owner can identify you as the accepted applicant. If there is no contact or other recognisable sign of joining within that time, the onboarding request will be treated as withdrawn. This is not a ban or a rejection.

Application withdrawal or privacy requests: You may ask to withdraw an application or request deletion of your application information by email at naxxapplicationteam@gmail.com. This is the general Naxxramas contact email for application questions, other enquiries, player support, and privacy or withdrawal requests. Do not email completed applications; submit those only through the private upload area on this page. Never include passwords or other sensitive details in general support messages.
