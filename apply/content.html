Application Privacy and Your Information

Naxxramas is a private World of Warcraft community operated by CosmicCuddle. The server owner is responsible for how application information is used. You can contact the owner at naxxapplicationteam@gmail.com with any application, support or privacy question.

What we collect and why

The official Word application asks for your preferred account username, email address, an optional Discord username and answers about your World of Warcraft experience and interests. We use this information to review your request to join, contact you with a decision and help arrange onboarding if accepted. The email address is needed to respond; Discord is optional. Do not include your real name, passwords, payment details or unnecessary sensitive information.

How decisions are made

Applications are reviewed by the server owner. Acceptance is a manual decision, not an automatic result of uploading a file. Our proposed lawful basis is the owner's legitimate interests in reviewing voluntary requests to join and maintaining a welcoming private gaming community; this basis requires final review before applications open. You can object to this use of your information by contacting us.

Who handles your application

You upload the completed Word file using Tally, which processes and stores it for the owner. The owner may download a copy for review. Tally and relevant email service providers also process submission and notification information. We do not publish completed applications or applicant answers on the Naxxramas website or in public Discord channels. Read Tally's privacy and data-processing information for details about its service providers, storage and transfers.

How long we keep it

The owner normally aims to respond within one or two days, but this is not guaranteed. An undecided application will be decided or closed within 90 days of submission; an application closed without a decision will be deleted by that deadline. After an acceptance or decline decision, the owner will delete the submitted application and any copies they hold within 30 days of the decision, including clearing the submission from Tally's Trash. Tally may retain provider backups temporarily in line with its own deletion procedures, so deletion from all backups may not be immediate. A minimal separate record of acceptance and joining status may be needed for onboarding, but it does not require keeping the original Word application.

Your choices and contact

You may withdraw an application or ask to access, correct or delete information you provided. You can also raise an objection or request that processing be restricted, where applicable. Email naxxapplicationteam@gmail.com, or privately message the owner on Discord if you can reach them there. You may also raise a data-protection concern with the UK Information Commissioner's Office where applicable. Do not email completed application documents or passwords; use the private upload area for finished applications once it opens.

What Happens Next?

The server owner will personally review applications and decide whether Naxxramas is suitable for the applicant. Submitting a form is not automatic acceptance and does not create a game account. Accounts are arranged manually after acceptance.

When the review is complete, the owner will reply by email, and will also reply through Discord when the applicant provided an optional Discord username. No fixed review turnaround is promised.

If accepted, the player receives the next steps privately, including the approved connection configuration as an attachment to the acceptance email. Passwords are never requested in the application.

After acceptance: You have one calendar month from your acceptance notification to show you still intend to join. You can confirm in whichever way suits you: reply to your acceptance email, send the owner a Discord message, join the Naxxramas Discord server, or log in to the WoW server. Any one of these is enough when the owner can identify you as the accepted applicant. If there is no contact or other recognisable sign of joining within that time, the onboarding request will be treated as withdrawn. This is not a ban or a rejection.

Application withdrawal or privacy requests: You may ask to withdraw an application or request deletion of your application information by email at naxxapplicationteam@gmail.com, or by private Discord message to the owner if you can reach them there. Either method is acceptable. This is the general Naxxramas contact email for application questions, other enquiries, player support, and privacy or withdrawal requests. Do not email completed applications; submit those only through the private upload area once applications open. Never send passwords or post personal information in public Discord channels.

Apply to Join — Frequently Asked Questions

These are anticipated applicant questions. Applications are not yet open.

Why do I need to apply?

Our hope is for Naxxramas to become a close-knit, welcoming community where players get to know one another, enjoy their time together and treat each other with respect. An application helps us learn what you enjoy about World of Warcraft and find people who are a good fit for both the server and the community we want to build. It is not a test of how experienced you are.

Do I need years of WoW experience?

No experience test is part of the planned application. We mainly want to understand your interests and what you enjoy about World of Warcraft.

Do I have to provide a real name or password?

No. The application asks for a preferred account username and an email address so the owner can contact you and arrange account creation after acceptance. A Discord username is optional. You do not need to share your real name, password or existing account login details.

Is Discord required?

No. Email is required as the main reply channel. Providing a Discord username is optional and allows an additional Discord reply.

Which application file do I upload?

Download the approved three-page Word application from the link above. Complete its fields and questions, save your copy, then upload it privately through the website once applications open. Only .docx files are planned for the upload.

When and how will I receive a reply?

The owner reviews each application manually and replies by email. If you provided Discord details, the owner also contacts you through Discord. There is no guaranteed response time. Once accepted, you have one calendar month to show you still intend to join. An email reply, Discord message to the owner, joining the Naxxramas Discord server, or logging into the game can each count when the owner can identify you. You do not need to do all four. Only if there is no recognisable contact or joining activity by then will the onboarding request be closed as withdrawn, not declined or banned.

How is the private connection file delivered?

If accepted, you receive the genuine approved connection file as an attachment to your private acceptance email. It will not be offered as a public download.

Will my application appear publicly?

No public application listing is planned. Before submissions open, the owner must verify private reviewer-only access, a privacy notice, and a manual retention/deletion procedure.
